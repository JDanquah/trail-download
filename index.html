What Trail does

Everything your plans need, in one place

Lay a plan out on the Canvas, the Timeline, a List or a Board, and see your days on the Calendar. Each one shows the same records.

Plans

Look at them your way

Give each task a date, its people and a checklist. See what is yours, what is everyone's, and what nobody has taken yet.

Tasks

Mine, Everyone, Unassigned

Turn your big plans into progress. Connect goals, milestones and everyday tasks, and see how far along you are.

Goals

Goals and milestones

Keep what comes in and what goes out beside the plan it belongs to. Every chart also says its numbers in words.

Money

Spending, income and funding

Write down the possibilities you have not committed to. Explore them, park them, and come back when you are ready.

Ideas

Exploring, Parked, Done

Invite people by @handle or email and choose what each one can do. Your records stay on your computer until you turn on sync.

People

You choose who sees what
